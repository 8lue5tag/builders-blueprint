# Phase 5 — Risk & Complexity Radar

1. **Top 3 technical risks.** For each: what it is, how bad (1-5), the mitigation. **At least one must be a product-assumption risk** — something the PRD assumes about users or behavior that, if wrong, breaks the architecture *or the UX* (the one that bites is usually "we assumed users would do X").
2. **Complexity hotspots** — parts disproportionately complex relative to user value. Defer any?
3. **What we're intentionally NOT building** — features or capabilities consciously deferred (backend *and* screens/states).

**Checkpoint:** "Risks acknowledged and mitigations agreed? Anything to de-scope or phase differently?"

> **Residual-risk check:** of everything in this session, what's the single decision you'd most want to revisit after the first week of development?
